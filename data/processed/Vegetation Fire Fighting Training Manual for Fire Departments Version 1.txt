Although the phenomenon of vegetation fires isnot new, their frequency, intensity, and impacts have changed significantly in recent decades. What was once considered a seasonal, localized problem can now cause widespread ecological and economic damage and increasingly endangers human life. These changes require a more comprehensive understanding ofthe underlying causes and dynamics of vegetation fires so that appropriate firefighting strategies canbe developed. Today, vegetation fires areno longer limited to remote rural areas. They increasingly threaten populated regions and urban fringe areas. This means that not only specialized emergency responders but also general firefighters must be familiar with the techniques and strategies for fighting such fires. Successful vegetation firefighting therefore requires an interdisciplinary approach that combines disciplines such as meteorology, botany, geography, and fire behavior. Adapting to these new circumstances requires not only increased operational readiness and flexibility, but also in-depth training for firefighters. This is precisely where this manual comes in. It offers a comprehensive guide that includes both theoretical knowledge and practical instructions. The goal isto optimally prepare emergency personnel for the diverse challenges of fighting vegetation fires andto provide them with the necessary know-how and appropriate tools to fight the fires effectively. Vegetation fires pose a serious threat to people, the environment, and infrastructure. With climate change and increasing droughts, fire departments and other emergency responders around the world are faced with the challenge of fighting increasingly frequent vegetation fires. This vegetation firefighting training manual for fire departments was developed to provide specific expertise and practical skills necessary to respond effectively to these growing challenges. This manual is aimed at both novice and experienced firefighters. It covers a wide range of topics, from basic definitions and the assessment of influencing factors to specific extinguishing techniques and tactical strategies inthe field. Particular attention is paid to combining theoretical knowledge with practical exercises and examples from real-life operational practice. This is intended to ensure that the content taught canbe not only understood theoretically but also applied in real-life situations. 1 Introduction

This includes, for example, directly controlling the flames with water or other extinguishing agents. Various scientific approaches and practical measures play a role in minimizing the risk of fire events and reducing their ecological impacts. 15.3 Prevention of future vegetation fires
In summary, preventing future vegetation fires requires a multifactorial approach that integrates technological innovations, forestry practices, and community engagement. Through coordinated and consistent implementation of these measures, fire risk canbe significantly reduced and ecosystem resilience strengthened. In addition to technological and forestry measures, raising public awareness isa key aspect of preventive strategies. Since many forest fires are triggered by human behavior, itis crucial to raise awareness ofthe risks and consequences of vegetation fires through education and outreach (SZG, 2023, p. 65). This includes imparting knowledge and rules of conduct in schools, as well as public campaigns. Significantly improve planning and awareness among emergency services (Rudolph, 2024, p. 69).
