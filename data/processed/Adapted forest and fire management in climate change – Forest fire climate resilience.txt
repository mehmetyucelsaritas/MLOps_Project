The project was launched to strengthen the resilience of forests and their managers tothe growing challenges of climate change and the rising risk of forest fires. To this end, internationally available knowledge wasto be made available to German practitioners, policymakers, and scientists. Frequent, sometimes disastrous forest fires areno longer just a southern European phenomenon; they have now reached Central Europe, including Germany. Hot, dry summers, storm damage, and beetle infestations increase vulnerability to forest fires. Therefore, adapting forests to this new risk is acute and urgent in Germany as well, requiring a strategy that goes beyond simply extinguishing fires.
